Who We Are

Engineering in Motion

Raven Racing is The SFEM Academy's student-run GreenPower F24 team. We design, build, and race a fully electric single-seat car — combining engineering, teamwork, and competition.

D

Design

Students engineer the chassis, drivetrain, and electrical systems from the ground up using CAD and real-world testing.

B

Build

The team fabricates, wires, and assembles the car in the shop, solving problems together as a crew.

R

Race

Raven Racing competes on the track against other schools in the GreenPower F24 electric racing series.

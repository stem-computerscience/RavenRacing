About Us

Who We Are

Raven Racing is the student-run GreenPower F24 team of The SFEM Academy, built on the belief that engineering is best learned by doing.

Our Mission

Design. Build. Race.

Raven Racing gives students hands-on experience in mechanical, electrical, and systems engineering by designing and racing a fully electric single-seat car in the GreenPower F24 competition. We compete as a team, and we learn as a team.

1

Hands-On Engineering

Every part of the car — chassis, drivetrain, wiring, and controls — is designed and built by students, guided by mentors.

2

Real Competition

We race against other schools in the GreenPower F24 electric racing series, testing our engineering against the clock and the track.

3

Team Culture

Design, Build, and Race sub-teams work together year-round, building both a car and a community.

The Program

What Is GreenPower F24?

GreenPower F24 is a national student engineering competition in which teams design, build, and race a single-seat electric car powered by a spec 24-volt motor. It's a low-cost, high-impact way for students to get real experience in STEM, teamwork, and motorsport.

A

Academy Roots

Raven Racing is proudly part of The SFEM Academy, and represents the school in regional and national GreenPower events.

S

Student-Led

From CAD design to race-day pit strategy, the team is run by students, for students, with support from faculty advisors and sponsors.

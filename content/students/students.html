Our Team

Meet the Students

Raven Racing is organized into five sub-teams that work together to design, build, and race our GreenPower F24 car.

Sub-Teams

How the Team Is Organized

Every student on Raven Racing belongs to a sub-team, each responsible for a different part of getting the car race-ready.

D

Design

CAD modeling, chassis and suspension engineering, and simulation.

E

Electrical

Wiring, battery management, motor controls, and telemetry.

F

Fabrication

Cutting, welding, and assembling the car in the shop.

R

Race & Pit Crew

Drivers, pit strategy, and race-day operations.

M

Marketing & Ops

Sponsorships, social media, event logistics, and fundraising.

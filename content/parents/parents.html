FAQ

Common Questions

Does my student need engineering experience to join?

No. Raven Racing welcomes students of all experience levels — sub-teams like Marketing & Ops and Race Crew don't require a technical background.

Are there costs involved?

The team is largely sponsor-funded. Any student fees or fundraising asks will be communicated directly by team advisors.

What safety measures are in place?

All shop and build activities are supervised by faculty advisors, with safety training required before students use tools or equipment.

How do I sign my student up?

Reach out to the team advisor using the contact info below to get an interest form and next steps.

Have Questions?

Reach out directly and a team advisor will get back to you.

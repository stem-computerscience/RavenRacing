Our Sponsors

Raven Racing is powered by the generosity of our sponsors. Their support funds the parts, tools, and travel that make our GreenPower F24 program possible.

Invest in Student Engineering

Help Students Design, Build, and Race

Raven Racing at STEM Academy at Bartlett is part of the international Greenpower F24 program. In a structured 8th grade course led by Mr. Robbins and Mr. Crow, students design, build, and race a single-seat electric car.

Real Engineering Experience

Students assemble a standardized kit of parts while following strict engineering and safety regulations. They test gear ratios, aerodynamics, and mechanical efficiency to manage battery power in endurance races. Working as engineers, builders, data analysts, and marketers, they develop teamwork, budgets, sponsorship proposals, and communication skills.

What Your Support Funds

Contributions support materials, tools, safety equipment, race entry fees, batteries, replacement parts, and team travel. Your sponsorship gives students hands-on learning opportunities and helps prepare future engineers, innovators, and leaders.

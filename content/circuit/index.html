Stock relay car: the motor is either on or off, just like the real F24. It cruises at about 20 mph on about 10 A (around 250 W) with a full battery, and slows to about 18 mph as the battery runs down. Launches pull up to 60 A, the main fuse rating. The battery acts like real AGM: hard launches drain extra charge, the car slows in the last 20%, and the yellow line marks 50%, where AGM batteries start to wear faster. Grass roughly halves your speed. Best laps and ghosts are saved on this device only.
